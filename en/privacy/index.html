Privacy policy

What data I collect, what I use it for and what rights you have over it.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Data controller

Marcos Rodríguez, Asola, 01400 Vantaa (Finland). Contact: contact@m4rkits.dev.

2. What data I process and why

Contact form. If you write to me through the form I process your name, your email, your business or website name (optional), your message and the type of service you select. I use them only to reply to your enquiry or prepare a quote. Legal basis: your consent, which you give by ticking the box, and, if you ask for a quote, steps taken before a possible contract.

WhatsApp. The button opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message. I receive no data until you send it. After that, the conversation is governed by WhatsApp's policy.

Visit statistics. I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures visits in aggregate, without cookies and without identifying you.

Technical data. The server that hosts the site (Cloudflare) may log technical data such as your IP address to keep the service secure and working.

Required data and automated decisions. Name, email and message are needed so I can reply: without them I cannot handle your enquiry. I make no automated decisions and build no profiles from your data.

3. Who else receives your data

Form messages go through EmailJS, the service that delivers them to my inbox and sends you the automatic confirmation. The site is hosted on Cloudflare. I do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

EmailJS is operated by a company in Singapore and its servers are in the United States. According to its privacy policy, for European users it applies safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Cloudflare may also process data outside the European Economic Area with equivalent safeguards.

5. How long I keep it

I keep your enquiry while I deal with it and, if we do not end up working together, for up to 12 months in case you pick the conversation back up. If we work together, I keep what the law requires for the legal periods. Statistics contain no personal data.

6. Your rights

You can ask me for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to contact@m4rkits.dev. Withdrawing it does not affect what was done before.

If you think I have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Finnish data protection authority (Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman) or to the one in your country.

7. Minors and security

This site is not aimed at minors. I apply reasonable measures to protect data, such as an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and access only from my own account.

8. Changes

If I change something important in this policy, I will update the date above.
